Delegates can register at any time, with slots allocated on a first-come-first-serve basis. Should your institution require special payment terms, please reach out to info@indabax.co.za.

## Supported delegates application

If you would like to join the IndabaX but do not have access to funding, you can apply for a __registration waiver__, __travel grant__ and/or __accommodation grant__. These are *highly competitive*, and allocated through an application process in which a panel of judges reviews your motivation for financial support against your existing involvement in the machine learning community. We try to communicate the outcome of your application around two months before the event, but slots may free up until right before the IndabaX starts. 

Applications are done through the Baobab app. Once your application is successful, you can register for the event [describe here how it works].

<div class="callout" markdown="1">
<div class="callout-text" markdown="1">
You can increase your chances of being selected by being as specific as possible in your application: we are looking for the things you are actually doing, not the things anyone could claim they want to do. 
<br><br>
__DON'T:__  _I am enthusiastic about growing AI research on the African continent and want to join the IndabaX to network with the best in the field._ 
<br><br> 
__DO:__  _I am a first year PhD student at UKZN in Prof Dube's group on image recognition for agricultural applications, but my supervisors refuse to pay for a conference unless I publish a paper which is still unfinished. I need to go to the IndabaX because I am looking for guidance on how to get open source satellite datasets, and to learn more about vision transformers which I will use in my next project. I am also quite passionate about training people in data science, and help organise the yearly ML workshop at my university where I serve as a tutor._

</div>

If your application for financial support is successful you will be granted one or more of the following options:

__Registration waiver__ means that you get full admission to the conference without paying a registration fee. 

__Accommodation grants__ usually consists of a bed in a shared room at the student residence. These are simple rooms where you have to bring your own towels and blankets. While the conference caters for lunch, coffees and some evening snacks, you will be responsible for all other meals during the duration of the event, as well as during travel times. 

__Travel grants__ will either consist of a seat in a dedicated bus, or a lump sum payment of the price equivalent of a bus ticket or petrol allowance into your account (in rare occasions we may decide to sponsor a flight ticket). In the latter case, you will be responsible for booking the journey. The travel grant might not cover all your expenses, and we also do not cover travel to and from the bus terminal or airport. Travel grants have to be reimbursed to us in case of no-shows without reason. Note that we do __not cover travel from outside of South Africa__ unless specifically negotiated.

Formal invitation letters for the presentation at embassies or work places can be self-generated in the Baobab system upon successful registration.
