## Financial support

If you would like to join the IndabaX but do not have access to funding, you can apply for some or all of these categories of financial support:

* __Registration waiver__ means that you get full admission to the conference without paying a registration fee. 

* __Accommodation grants__ usually consists of a bed in a shared room at the student residence. These are simple rooms where you have to bring your own towels and blankets. While the conference caters for lunch, coffees and some evening snacks, you will be responsible for all other meals during the duration of the event, as well as during travel times. 

* __Travel grants__ will either consist of a seat in a dedicated bus, or a lump sum payment of the price equivalent of a bus ticket or petrol allowance into your account (in rare occasions we may decide to sponsor a flight ticket). In the latter case, you will be responsible for booking the itinerary. Although we try our best to allocate funding fairly, the travel grant might not cover all your expenses, as we usually do not cover travel to and from the bus terminal or airport. Travel grants have to be reimbursed to us in case of no-shows without reason. Note that we stricltly do __not cover travel from outside of South Africa__ unless specifically negotiated.

Financial support is *highly competitive*, and allocated through an application process in which a panel of judges take into account your motivation for financial support, the level of support you ask for and your existing involvement in the machine learning community. We try to communicate the outcome of your application around two months before the event, but slots may free up until right before the IndabaX starts. 

Applications for financial support are submitted through the Baobab platform. Once your application is successful, you get a customised link to register for the event.
