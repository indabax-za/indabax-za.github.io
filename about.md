The Deep Learning IndabaX is a locally-organised [Deep Learning Indaba](deeplearningindaba.com) conference aimed at strengthening machine learning in South Africa. We are part of a huge network: There are almost as many IndabaX events as there are countries in Africa, ranging from 1-day introductory workshops to full blown conferences like ours.

## Event format

Our IndabaX is a hybrid between a traditional academic conference, hackathon, summer school and industry expo. This format creates spaces to connect: For labs to find top achievers, for students to fall in love with a research topic, for companies to find South Africa's talent in machine learning, and for enthusiasts to become part of the machine learning community. 

## The Indaba spirit

The Indaba movement is based on inclusivity and kindness. We want to create a community in which everyone feels they can join authentically and free of fear, in order to perform at their best. The code of conduct can be read [here](link), and violations must be reported to XXX.

## Funding

The IndabaX is funded entirely by our partners. Every year, a team of volunteers puts together the means to hold the event in countless hours of hard work and utter dedication. No one gets paid. The largest share of our budget is invested into financial support for delegates, such as fee waivers, accommodation and travel grants.

Find ways to support our effort [here](link-to-page).
