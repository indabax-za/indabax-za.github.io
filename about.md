The Deep Learning IndabaX is a locally-organised [Deep Learning Indaba](http://www.deeplearningindaba.com) conference aimed at strengthening machine learning in South Africa.

We are part of a huge network: There are almost as many IndabaX events as there are countries in Africa, ranging from single-day introductory workshops to full blown conferences like ours. 

## The Indaba spirit

The Indaba movement is based on inclusivity, excellence and kindness. We want to create a community in which everyone feels they can join authentically in order to perform at their best. We believe that what we are is what we build: the way we treat others will show in the technology we create for the world.

## Event format

The IndabaX conference is usually a hybrid between a traditional academic conference, hackathon, summer school and industry expo. This format creates spaces to connect: For labs to find top achievers, for students to fall in love with a research topic, for companies to find South Africa's talent in machine learning, and for enthusiasts to become part of the machine learning community.

## Funding

The IndabaX is funded entirely by our partners. Every year, a team of volunteers raises money for the event. No one gets paid, and many of our speakers fund their own stay. The largest share of our budget is invested into financial support for delegates, such as fee waivers, accommodation and travel grants.

## Leadership and roles

The _Deep Learning IndabaX South Africa NPC_ (registration number K2021781317) is a registered Non Profit Company and Public Benefit Organisation with 18A tax exemption status. Together with an executive team of seasoned IndabaX veterans, the directors of the NPC form the _steering committee_ that takes strategic decisions and ensures compliance.
