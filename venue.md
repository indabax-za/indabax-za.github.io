## Accommodation

### On-campus housing

Students who successfully apply for an accommodation grant will be hosted in shared rooms in the student residence. 

Limited additional rooms for self-funded delegates are available, and details will be shared closer in time here. We recommend early bookings, as the popular Arts Festival takes place in the week after the conference.
