The Deep Learning IndabaXS events in South Africa are locally hosted gatherings in the Indaba spirit, which are tailored to a specific campus, institution, or community. These are typically structured as a half-day or full-day programme, but are shaped by local priorities and interests, with content that speaks directly to the challenges faced in that context. 

To host an IndabaXS, reach out to **outreach@indabax.co.za**.

### 14 May 2026 - UWC Webinar

<strong> AI and Cybersecurity in the Evolving Job Landscape: Opportunities, Risks, and the Future of Work</strong>

The webinar was hosted in collaboration with the Deep Learning IndabaX South Africa, UWC Women in Computer Science Society, UWC Geekulcha Student Society, and the UWC IT Society. The session brought together speakers and participants to discuss how artificial intelligence and cybersecurity are changing careers, skills demand, workplace risks, and future opportunities. It also created a space for students to engage with practitioners and reflect on how they can prepare for emerging technology-driven careers.
