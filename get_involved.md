## Become a sponsor

To learn more about sponsorship opportunities, read our sponsor prospectus or reach out to **fundraising@indabax.co.za**. 

[Sponsor prospectus →](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Z6EEHwRx8PBAfFV8oIjWHple2AdTJCM5/view?usp=drive_link){: .button}

*Note that the Deep Learning IndabaX South Africa is a Section 18A Non Profit Company, and donations are tax-deductible.*

## Contribute to the scientific programme 

We welcome suggestions for contributions to the scientific programme by experienced researchers and AI professionals who want to share their work. Send your ideas to **programme@indabax.co.za**.

## Host a hackathon challenge

Partner with our hackathon team to run your challenge at the IndabaX, or sponsor prices for the best submissions. The hackathon usually runs over 2 nights and consists of beginners and advanced tracks. Contact **hackathon@indabax.co.za** for more information.

## Exhibit your organisation

We often have opportunities for companies and institutes to exhibit their work. Reach out to **partners@indabax.co.za** to find out about this year's platforms to network.

## Join the organising team 

Organising the Deep Learning IndabaX is a huge effort that we all do after-hours, and we invite you to join our kind, professional and tireless organising committee. We usually assemble the team towards the end of the previous year through brief interviews, but will also consider late volunteers. You can join specific efforts, such as the local organisation, poster session, website support, fundraising, programme, accounting or hackathon teams.

To indicate your interest, send an email to **info@indabax.co.za**.
