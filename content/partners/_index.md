Our **partners** are more than just funders. They are the most avid believers in our South African machine learning community. 
They are the startups pushing boundaries and expanding local expertise. They are the people who advocate within their businesses 
to do more. They are institutions that share our vision. Our partners help to grow the IndabaX year-on-year.
