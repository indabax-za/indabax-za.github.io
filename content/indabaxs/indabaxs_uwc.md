**Event Type:** Webinar

**Event Title:** AI and Cybersecurity in the Evolving Job Landscape: Opportunities, Risks, and the Future of Work

**Description:** The webinar was hosted in collaboration with Deep Learning IndabaX South Africa, UWC Women in Computer Science Society, UWC Geekulcha Student Society, and the UWC IT Society. The session brought together speakers and participants to discuss how artificial intelligence and cybersecurity are changing careers, skills demand, workplace risks, and future opportunities. It also created a space for students to engage with practitioners and reflect on how they can prepare for emerging technology-driven careers.
