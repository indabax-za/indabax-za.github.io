### Overview

IndabaXS at the University of the Western Cape is a locally hosted initiative that supports the growth of data science, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and digital technology capacity at the campus and community level. The chapter forms part of the broader Deep Learning IndabaX South Africa movement. It creates a practical and accessible space where students, researchers, academics, industry practitioners, and community partners can learn, share ideas, and build networks.

At UWC, IndabaXS aims to make AI and data science more accessible to students from different disciplines. The chapter promotes practical exposure to emerging technologies, responsible use of AI, data literacy, cybersecurity awareness, and career readiness for the future world of work.

---
### What the UWC IndabaXS Chapter Aims to Do


**The UWC IndabaXS chapter aims to:**

+ · Build awareness of artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, cybersecurity, and emerging digital technologies.

+ · Create inclusive learning spaces for students, researchers, staff, and young professionals.

+ · Support practical skills development through talks, tutorials, panel discussions, showcases, and networking sessions.

+ · Encourage responsible and ethical use of AI and data.

+ · Promote data literacy as a key graduate and professional skill.

+ · Connect UWC students with AI, data science, cybersecurity, and technology communities in South Africa.

+ · Strengthen collaboration between students, academic departments, student societies, industry partners, and national AI networks.
